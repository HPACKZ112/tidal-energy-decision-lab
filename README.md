# tidal-energy-decision-lab
Main question: Which turbine configuration offers the strongest project economics under clearly stated engineering, resource and cost assumptions.
Will be comparing 2, 3, and 4 blade designs with a diameter of 20m including a hub radius of 2 meters.
In order to determine which is most economically viable I will compare the cost, time for construction, energy production and instillation cost.
For the initial scope I will use a simplified power model to compare annual energy estimates with capital and operating costs to find investment returns and sensitivity analysis
